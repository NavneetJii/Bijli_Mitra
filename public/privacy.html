Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BijliMitra ("BijliMitra", "we", "us", "our"), an undertaking of Kashvi Enterprises, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and electrician-booking service (the "Service") at bijlimitraservice.in.

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, and password (stored in encrypted/hashed form), collected when you sign up as a customer or when an administrator creates an electrician account on your behalf.
  • Authentication information via Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google to create or sign in to your account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Service location details: the district, city/village, landmark, and pincode you select or provide when booking a service, so an electrician can be assigned and reach you.
  • Booking and order details: the services you request, the date/time of booking, order status, service notes, and any items or charges added by the assigned electrician.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our third-party payment gateway partner, Cashfree Payments. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank account details on our servers — Cashfree handles and secures this information directly. We retain only transaction references, amounts, and payment status needed to confirm and reconcile your booking.
  • Communications: if you contact us for support, we retain the content of that communication to respond to you.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as browser type and log data generated automatically through our hosting and authentication providers, used for security and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and to verify your identity.
  • To process bookings, route service requests to an appropriate electrician, and enable the electrician to complete the job at your location.
  • To process payments securely through Cashfree Payments and to generate accurate bills, including applicable GST.
  • To send transactional communications, such as sign-up confirmation, password-reset, and booking-status emails.
  • To provide customer support and resolve disputes relating to a booking or payment.
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the Service, and to prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To comply with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as necessary to operate the Service:

  • Assigned electrician: your name, phone number, and service location are shared with the electrician assigned to your booking, solely to carry out the service.
  • Cashfree Payments: payment details you enter during checkout are transmitted directly to Cashfree to process the transaction, under Cashfree's own privacy and security practices.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase for database, authentication, and backend infrastructure, and Resend for transactional email delivery. These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually restricted from using it for any other purpose.
  • Google: if you sign in with Google, Google processes the authentication request in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required to do so by law, or in response to a valid request from a public authority.

4. Data Retention

We retain account, booking, and payment-reference information for as long as your account is active and thereafter for as long as necessary to comply with our legal, accounting, and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account as described below.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and Row-Level Security on our database — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may access and update your account information from within the app at any time.
  • You may request that we delete your account and associated personal information by contacting us using the details below, subject to any information we are legally required to retain.
  • You may unlink Google sign-in and use email/password authentication instead, or vice versa.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for use by adults booking electrician services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the Service after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
